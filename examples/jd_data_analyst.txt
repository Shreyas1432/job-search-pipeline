Data Analyst - Finance Reporting
Contoso Financial, Dublin (hybrid)

About the role
You will build and maintain finance reporting, analyse large datasets and keep the data accurate.

Responsibilities
- Build Power BI dashboards and reports, including data modelling and DAX measures.
- Write SQL to extract, transform and validate data from the data warehouse.
- Reconcile financial data and investigate data quality issues.
- Document data definitions and track work in Jira.

Requirements (essential)
- 2+ years of experience in a data analyst or reporting role.
- Strong SQL, including window functions.
- Power BI with DAX and data modelling.
- Advanced Excel.

Desirable
- Python (pandas) for data analysis.
- Experience with Snowflake or dbt.
- Tableau.

Salary: EUR 62,000 - EUR 72,000
